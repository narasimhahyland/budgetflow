Product overview

Allocate a budget once. Control it everywhere.

BudgetFlow Enterprise is a budget delegation and control platform. Leadership allocates a budget once, and it flows through the organization with live visibility, approvals within clear limits and a complete audit trail.

It works alongside the card, expense, HR, planning and finance systems the company already uses, so teams keep their tools and leadership gains one view and one set of controls.

Why it exists

The problem it solves

Budgets are set at the top, but the money is spent by many people in many places.

  • Budget owners cannot see what they have left, so questions go to finance and decisions wait.
  • Budgets are split by email and spreadsheet, and per-person programs are recalculated by hand as headcount changes.
  • Mid-year cuts take weeks and can break events that are already booked.
  • Events across locations need money from several leaders, agreed informally and hard to audit later.

How it works

From annual budget to closed books

One budget, set once at the top, moves through the organization with checks at every step.

  1. Set the annual budget

    Leadership approves the company budget once.

  2. Allocate to regions

    Each share is checked against what is available above it.

  3. Delegate to locations

    Regional managers share budget by headcount or fixed amounts.

  4. Plan an event

    An organizer requests extra funds or co-funding from another leader.

  5. Approvals route automatically

    The right people approve within their limits, from email, chat or mobile.

  6. Spend is matched

    Card and expense transactions are matched to budgets and reconciled.

  7. Leadership stays in control

    Freeze or cut any budget, with the impact shown first.

  8. Finance closes the period

    Numbers lock, with a full audit trail behind every figure.

What it does

Features at a glance

Eleven capabilities that cover the full budget lifecycle, from planning to audit.

01

Budget planning and allocation

  • Set the annual budget top-down and allocate it across regions, departments and locations.
  • Per-person programs calculate each location's share from headcount and follow people as they join or leave.
  • Start from templates, import an existing plan or roll last year forward.
  • Plan versions keep the original, current and forecast figures side by side.
02

Live visibility and dashboards

  • Every owner sees available, committed and spent amounts as they change.
  • One company-wide definition of available, so every report agrees.
  • Drill down from the company total to a single event or line.
03

Leadership controls

  • Freeze, reduce or set a budget to zero in one step, always with a reason.
  • The impact is shown before confirming: affected teams, open commitments and pending requests.
  • Commitments already made are honored; new requests stop.
04

Requests and approvals

  • Requests route to the right approvers automatically, within limits set by role and amount.
  • Approvers see the budget left after approval and why the request came to them.
  • Approve from email, chat or a phone.
  • Delegation and escalation keep things moving when people are away.
05

Co-funding between leaders

  • The Inter-Director Budget Contribution Request lets one leader ask another to fund part of an event.
  • The contributor approves in full, approves part or declines with a reason.
  • A co-funding board shows each contributor's share, the spend against it and what is returned.
06

Events and engagement

  • Plan events across locations, with costs split fairly by location and team.
  • Invitations, RSVPs, attendee needs, vendor quotes and checklists in one place.
  • Surveys, return-on-investment scorecards, recognition and polls show what each event achieved.
07

Spend tracking and reconciliation

  • Actual spend arrives from the card, expense and finance tools already in use.
  • Transactions are matched to the right budget and event automatically.
  • Exceptions go to a short review queue, and finance signs off each month against the books.
08

Finance close and forecasting

  • Close each period and lock the numbers that went to the board.
  • Forecasts carry owner commentary wherever results move away from plan.
  • Year-end rules decide what carries forward and what lapses.
  • Costs include tax that cannot be recovered, so budgets show the true cost.
09

Insights and AI assistance

  • Ask questions in plain language and get answers limited to your own budgets.
  • Early warnings of under- and overspend, suggested allocations and alerts on unusual activity.
  • AI suggests; people decide. It never approves or moves money on its own.
10

Governance and audit

  • A tamper-evident history of every change, with before and after values and the reason.
  • Role-based access: people see only the budgets they are responsible for.
  • One-click evidence packs and clear data retention rules.
11

Connected ecosystem

  • Connectors to HR, finance, planning, card and expense tools.
  • Reports and widgets for BI tools and intranets, delivered on a schedule by email and chat.
  • Native mobile apps for approvals, balances and receipts on the go.

Who uses it

Roles and how they use it

Each person sees only the budgets they are responsible for, with the actions their role allows.

Leadership and CEO

Sees

Company-wide budget health, spend by region, and fairness and participation across locations.

Does

  • Sets the annual budget and approves new money
  • Freezes, reduces or zeroes budgets with the impact shown first
  • Reviews leadership summaries and the board pack

Finance: Controller, Admin, FP&A

Sees

Balances, commitments and actual spend tied back to the company's books.

Does

  • Imports the plan and sets approval limits
  • Reconciles spend and signs off each month
  • Closes periods, runs forecasts and the year-end

HR, People Ops and Employee Experience

Sees

Per-person programs, participation and reach by location.

Does

  • Sets up programs from templates with per-person rates
  • Keeps headcount current so allocations follow people
  • Nudges teams before money expires and reads survey results

Regional and location managers

Sees

Their own budget, what is left and upcoming events.

Does

  • Delegate budget to locations and teams
  • Plan events and request extra funds or co-funding
  • Approve from email, chat or mobile

Department heads and budget owners

Sees

Each request with the budget left after approval and why it was routed to them.

Does

  • Approve, approve part or decline
  • Delegate approvals while away
  • Set alert thresholds on their budgets

Event organizers

Sees

The event budget, each contributor's share, RSVPs and cost per person.

Does

  • Build events from templates
  • Collect RSVPs, attendee needs and vendor quotes
  • Capture receipts and close the event, returning any surplus

Employees

Sees

Their team's remaining budget and upcoming events.

Does

  • Suggest and vote on activities
  • Submit simple requests and receipts

Auditors and analysts

Sees

The complete, tamper-evident history and read-only reports.

Does

  • Trace any amount to who approved it and why
  • Export evidence packs
  • Review exceptions and unusual patterns

Administrators

Sees

Users, roles, policies and connected systems.

Does

  • Manage access by role and part of the organization
  • Configure policies and approval rules
  • Connect HR, finance and spend systems
