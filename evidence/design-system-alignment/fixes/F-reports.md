## Shared requests (outside my scope)

1. **`scripts/visual/capture.mjs`.** `.bf-table-scroll` (`overflow-x: auto`) also computes `overflow-y: auto`, so tall table wrappers counted as page scrollers and their intended sideways scroll was flagged as blocking (38 findings in fix-F4). By fix-F5 this no longer appears, so it may already be fixed; please confirm the criterion excludes horizontal-only scrollers.
2. **Shell header.** `div.bf-header__actions` overflowed `main` by 2–17px at 412 depending on the role (fix-F2: 086, 015a, 080e forbidden). It is no longer flagged in fix-F5.
3. **`libs/web/ui/src/styles/kpi-card.scss`.** `.bf-kpis` at `minmax(200px)` clips "USD 103,000.00" at 24px/700. I used 240px locally in `bf-report-kpis` and on 087a; suggest 240px globally.
4. **`libs/web/ui/src/styles/page-layout.scss`.** `.bf-filters` has `padding: 8px 20px 0`, so the last field touches the card's bottom border on mobile (N9).
5. **Contracts and API.** Board-pack `page.totals` mixes currencies (016a). Commitment hygiene compares USD with INR (085a). Report rows should carry the entity code separately from the label. Check prose should send money as money objects ("2000.00 USD"). Report copy has raw enums ("open", "earmark", "cross source duplicate", threshold "2500").
6. **`libs/web/ui`.** An exported `findSpendStatus(kind, status)` would replace my generic-fallback detection in `reportStatus()`.
7. **`screens.json`.** Proposed accounting entries are in `tmp/audit/fixes/F-screens.json`. The 087 "Forecast at year end" and 080e break-glass `request-dialog` states should be re-baselined because their visuals changed.
