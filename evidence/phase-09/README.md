## Invariants Proven (`docs/testing/invariant-scenarios.md`)

- **Invariant 44:** Event templates, recurring series and cloning never reserve or move money; series occurrences in Closed or Locked fiscal periods are rejected (`EVENT_DATE_IN_CLOSED_PERIOD`); a dry run creates nothing and each created occurrence is an ordinary draft with its own code and no own funds.
- **Invariant 45:** Invitations enforce capacity with a waitlist and automatic promotion on cancellation; individual dietary, allergy, accessibility, travel and photo-consent needs are visible only to the event's organizers and the employee themselves while contributors and finance receive aggregated counts only; caterer exports with names require food-safety confirmation and write an audit entry.
- **Invariant 46:** Vendor quotes convert to the budget currency at the locked plan rate; only Approved or Preferred vendors can be selected (`VENDOR_NOT_APPROVED`, `VENDOR_BLOCKED`); expired quotes (`QUOTE_EXPIRED`) and quotes above event available (`EXCEEDS_EVENT_AVAILABLE`) are rejected; selecting a non-lowest quote requires a written reason (`REASON_REQUIRED`); selecting a quote creates the event commitment, declines the other quotes of its request and records any non-refundable deposit on the commitment, which Phase 6 commitment hygiene charges as the cancellation fee.
- **Invariant 47:** Per-head cost and contributor shares recalculate from finalized attendance (largest remainder); applying a new split is rejected when any contributor's share exceeds their approved contribution (`SHARE_EXCEEDS_CONTRIBUTION`) and otherwise re-posts commitment funding lines as linked reversals and new postings in the open period; post-event surveys enforce a 5-response minimum before showing results (`SURVEY_ALREADY_ANSWERED` on duplicate answers); the ROI scorecard reconciles planned vs actual per-attendee cost, no-show waste, participation, feedback and surplus returns with FX shown separately.
