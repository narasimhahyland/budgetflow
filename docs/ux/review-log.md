## Decisions

- 2026-11-04: dark theme deferred; design and export light theme only. Dark screens already generated stay in Stitch but are not exported or specified.
- 2026-11-04: one company, Hyland. Designs show the company name only (static sidebar label); no tenant or organization switcher. "Acme Global" is replaced everywhere. Sample people, amounts and vendors stay fictional; cities are real office cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Westlake, London).
- 2026-11-04: all budgets in USD and reporting fixed to USD (no currency chip or switcher). Transactions and actuals may show INR or EUR with the USD amount at the locked plan rate; FX effect is shown separately.
- 2026-11-04: executive Available = Authorized - pending requests - approved commitments - actual (company-wide); budget drill-downs show "Available under this budget's rules" with a tooltip explaining any difference.
- 2026-11-04: dashboards and reports have an Export menu only (CSV, XLSX, PDF), no Share button; scheduled delivery stays.
- 2026-11-04: the clickable design preview is approved for publishing; its live address is in [preview.md](preview.md).
